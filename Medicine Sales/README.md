## 🚀 Key Features & KPIs
* **Financial Overview:** Track Total Quantity (23K), Total Revenue (6M ₼), Total Profit (5M ₼), Total Cost, and Profit Margin (81.80%).
* **Monthly Trends:** Comparative monthly performance tracking for revenue and cost metrics.
* **Geographical & Demographic Insights:** Sales distribution across countries (Canada, Australia, UK, etc.) and analysis by age groups and user categories.
* **Product-Level Breakdown:** Specific tracking of drug/medicine revenue rankings.
